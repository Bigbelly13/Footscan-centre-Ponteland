Services & Pricing

From your first 3D scan to custom orthotics and ongoing podiatry care — here's everything we offer, and what it costs.

Our signature service

3D Foot & Gait Scanning

Using a high-resolution pressure plate, we capture how weight moves across your feet both standing still and walking. The scan reveals pressure hot-spots, arch behaviour, and asymmetries between your left and right foot that aren't visible to the naked eye.

  • Static and dynamic (walking) pressure mapping
  • Digital 3D model of your arch and foot shape
  • A copy of your results to take away, explained in person

Delivered in partnership with Momentum Sports Injury Clinic

Footscan appointments are carried out via Momentum Sports Injury Clinic's own Footscan system and booking platform.

Made for your feet

Custom Orthotics

If your scan reveals a biomechanical issue that's contributing to pain, we can design and manufacture bespoke orthotic insoles built directly from your scan data — no generic templates.

  • Built from your individual 3D scan, not a standard mould
  • Options for everyday shoes, sports trainers or work boots
  • Free fitting check and adjustment within 4 weeks

Delivered in partnership with Momentum Sports Injury Clinic

Custom orthotics are designed and fitted through Momentum Sports Injury Clinic's gait analysis and orthotics service.

For runners & athletes

Running & Sports Assessment

Combining pressure-plate data with slow-motion video, we assess your running or sporting gait to identify inefficiencies and injury risk factors — then recommend footwear, training or orthotic changes.

  • Treadmill gait analysis with slow-motion video review
  • Footwear recommendations tailored to your gait pattern
  • Injury-prevention exercise plan included
Root-cause analysis

Biomechanical Assessment

Foot problems often start higher up the chain. Our full biomechanical assessment looks at your ankles, knees, hips and lower back to understand how they influence — and are influenced by — the way you walk.

  • Full lower-limb joint range and alignment check
  • Muscle strength and flexibility screening
  • Written report suitable for GP or physio referral
Preventative care

Diabetic & At-Risk Foot Care

Regular foot checks are essential for anyone with diabetes or reduced circulation. We screen for early warning signs and provide routine care to help prevent complications before they start.

  • Circulation and nerve sensitivity testing
  • Risk-graded review with a personalised check-up schedule
  • Nail and skin care carried out safely by registered staff
Everyday care

General Podiatry & Chiropody

Routine foot health care for the whole family — from nail trimming and hard skin removal to verruca and fungal nail treatment.

  • Nail care, corns, calluses and hard skin removal
  • Verruca and fungal nail treatment
  • Ingrown toenail assessment and treatment

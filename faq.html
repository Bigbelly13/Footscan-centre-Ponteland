Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a footscan® assessment, Phits orthotics or biomechanical assessment.

A Footscan Assessment uses the footscan® pressure plate to measure how weight and pressure move through your feet while standing and walking. A Biomechanical Assessment is a broader gait analysis, looking at how your ankles, knees, hips and posture work together, and is a good starting point if you're not sure a foot-specific scan is what you need.

No. Footscan Centre Ponteland offers three services only: footscan® gait analysis, Phits 3D-printed orthotics and biomechanical assessment. We don't provide podiatry, nail or skin care, or diabetic at-risk foot screening — if you need those services, your GP practice or a registered podiatrist can help.

Please bring the shoes you wear most often (including sports trainers, if relevant) and any existing insoles or orthotics, so we can compare them against your footscan® results.

No referral is needed — you can book directly. If you have a referral letter from your GP or physiotherapist, please bring it along or email it to us in advance.

A Footscan Assessment is £90 (around 45 minutes), Phits Orthotics are £240 per pair, and a Biomechanical Assessment is £80 (around 60 minutes). Each service is booked and charged separately via Momentum Sports Injury Clinic. See the Services & Pricing page for full details.

Many major health insurers cover physiotherapy and sports rehabilitation assessments — please check directly with your provider. We can provide an itemised invoice for you to claim back.

Phits orthotics are typically ready for collection or fitting within 2–3 weeks of your footscan® appointment, once your design has been 3D printed.

We're based at Unit 4, The Green House, Meadowfield Industrial Estate, Ponteland, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE20 9SD, inside Momentum Sports Injury Clinic. Free on-site parking is available. See the Contact page for a map.

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule, so we can offer the slot to another patient.

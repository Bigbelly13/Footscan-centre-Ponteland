Gait-Related Conditions

We don't diagnose or treat these conditions directly — but a footscan® assessment or biomechanical assessment can often show whether the way you walk or run is a contributing factor, which is useful information to bring to your GP or physio.

Plantar Fasciitis

Heel and arch pain, often worst with your first steps in the morning. A footscan® assessment can show how pressure loads through your arch and heel during walking, which helps when considering supportive Phits orthotics.

See footscan® assessment →

Shin Splints

Pain along the shin bone, common in runners increasing mileage. Dynamic footscan® data can highlight excess pronation or uneven loading through your stride that may be worth discussing with a clinician.

See footscan® assessment →

Overpronation

Excess inward rolling of the foot during walking or running. This is exactly what dynamic footscan® measurement is designed to capture and quantify, frame by frame through your stride.

See footscan® assessment →

Knee Pain Linked to Gait

Knee pain (including patellofemoral pain) can sometimes be influenced by how your foot loads and rotates lower down the chain. A biomechanical assessment uses gait analysis to look at the whole lower limb, not just your feet, to help build that picture.

See biomechanical assessment →

Hip & Lower-Back Pain

Postural and gait asymmetries can sometimes contribute to hip and lower-back discomfort. A biomechanical assessment reviews posture and movement patterns alongside your footscan® results for a fuller picture.

See biomechanical assessment →

Achilles Tendinopathy

Pain and stiffness at the back of the heel, often aggravated by running. Understanding how your heel strikes and how load transfers through toe-off can help inform footwear and orthotic choices alongside your treatment plan.

See footscan® assessment →

This page is for general information only and is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment from a GP, physiotherapist or podiatrist. Footscan Centre Ponteland provides footscan® gait analysis, Phits orthotics and biomechanical assessment only — we do not diagnose or treat medical conditions.

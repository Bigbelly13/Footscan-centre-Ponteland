What we do

Foot health services under one roof

Every visit starts with understanding how your feet actually move — then we build a plan around it.

footscan® Gait Analysis

High-speed pressure-plate scanning — up to 48,384 sensors at 500 scans/sec — captures how your feet load and move through every phase of your stride. Delivered via Momentum Sports Injury Clinic.

Learn more →

Phits 3D-Printed Orthotics

Insoles 3D printed from your footscan® data, accurate to 0.1mm and less than half the weight of a traditional orthotic. Delivered via Momentum Sports Injury Clinic.

Learn more →

Running & Sports Assessment

Slow-motion video and pressure analysis to reduce injury risk and improve efficiency for runners and athletes.

Learn more →

Biomechanical Assessment

Full lower-limb assessment to identify the root cause of knee, hip, back or foot pain linked to how you walk. Delivered via Momentum Sports Injury Clinic.

Learn more →

Diabetic & At-Risk Foot Care

Routine screening and preventative care for diabetic patients and anyone with reduced circulation or sensation.

How it works

Your first scan, step by step

A typical initial assessment takes around 45 minutes, start to finish.

1

Book online

Choose a service and pick a time that suits you — most appointments are available within a week.

2

Consultation

We talk through your symptoms, footwear, activity levels and any previous injuries.

3

3D scan

Step onto our pressure plate for a static and dynamic scan of how your feet bear weight and move.

4

Personalised plan

We explain your results in plain language and agree a plan — from exercises to custom orthotics.
